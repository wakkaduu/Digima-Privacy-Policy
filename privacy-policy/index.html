Digima Messenger CRM Privacy Policy

How the CRM may collect, use, disclose and protect personal information in connection with Facebook Page inquiries.

1. Overview and scope

Digima Web Solutions Inc. (“Digima,” “we,” “us”) provides Digima Messenger CRM, a tool designed to help authorized business representatives manage customer messages sent to connected Facebook Pages. This notice explains the processing activities associated with that service.

Company: Digima Web Solutions Inc.Effective date: pending approvalLast updated: November 13, 2026 (draft)

Company review required. This is a proposed policy. The company's approved privacy officer must confirm its legal bases, hosting, security implementation, retention rules and contact channels before it is published.

Where Digima controls a Facebook Page, it may determine the purposes and means of processing inquiries on that Page. Where Digima provides software on behalf of another Page-owning business, the Page owner may be the personal information controller and Digima may act as a processor, subject to applicable arrangements. The responsible party depends on the actual business relationship.

2. Information processed

Depending on the connected Page, the functions enabled, and what Meta makes available through authorized access, the CRM may process:

  • Messenger and Page data: Page names and identifiers, Page-scoped customer identifiers, message content, conversation history, timestamps, delivery-related information and attachments.
  • Customer-provided information: contact details, product or service questions, purchase inquiries and other information voluntarily included in messages.
  • Authorized staff data: staff login identifiers, access roles, Page assignments, conversation assignments and related administrative actions.
  • Technical records: authentication, system activity, error and security logs needed to operate and protect the CRM.

Customers should avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in Messenger conversations.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Information may be used to receive and respond to inquiries, organize conversations across authorized Pages, assign cases to staff, manage follow-up, send permitted replies and attachments, maintain records, protect the service and comply with lawful obligations.

Processing must rely on a valid legal basis under applicable law, which may include consent, contractual necessity, a legal obligation or another basis applicable to the specific activity.

Company confirmation needed: legal basis for each processing activity

A Messenger inquiry is not automatically treated as permission for unrelated marketing. Any marketing, profiling or automated decision-making requires separate assessment and notice as applicable.

4. Who may access or receive information

Information may be accessible to the relevant Page-owning business and its authorized representatives, Digima personnel supporting an approved service, appropriately contracted technical service providers, and public authorities when legally required. Meta also processes data through its Messenger platform.

Staff access should be limited by role and authorized Page assignment. Service providers and disclosures must be documented according to the company's approved arrangements.

Company confirmation needed: vendors and recipient categories

5. Security measures

The CRM has been designed with authentication, role-based access, Page isolation, protected credentials and security logging. Technical safeguards are subject to deployment and operational verification and do not eliminate all risks.

Digima must confirm the security measures actually in operation for the deployed service, including hosting controls, account administration, backup access and incident procedures, before publishing this notice.

6. Retention and secure deletion

Personal information must be retained only as long as necessary for its disclosed purpose and applicable legal or business obligations. When retention is no longer justified, it should be deleted, anonymized or otherwise handled securely under approved procedures.

  • Messages and conversation records: Retention period to be confirmed
  • Attachments and customer information: Retention period to be confirmed
  • Staff, technical and security records: Retention period to be confirmed
  • Backups and deletion propagation: Procedure to be confirmed

7. Your privacy rights

Individuals have rights under applicable privacy law, which may include rights to be informed, access information, request correction, object to certain processing, request erasure or blocking where applicable, data portability where supported by law, and seek appropriate remedies or complain to the Philippine National Privacy Commission.

Rights requests may require reasonable identity verification and may be subject to lawful restrictions.

8. How to request deletion

To request deletion or access concerning a Messenger conversation, individuals should identify the Facebook Page they contacted and the approximate conversation date, without submitting passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

The company must confirm and publish a functioning privacy request channel, identity-verification process and deletion workflow before this notice goes live.

Company-approved privacy email: not yet provided

See the Data Deletion Instructions for the proposed procedure and requirements.

9. Third-party platforms and international transfers

Facebook Messenger is operated by Meta. Use of Facebook and Messenger is also subject to Meta's Privacy Policy. The CRM may use approved hosting and infrastructure providers, depending on its production deployment.

If information is stored or processed outside the Philippines, the applicable locations and transfer safeguards should be disclosed and assessed according to Philippine law.

Company confirmation needed: hosting region, vendors and transfer safeguards

Once approved, Digima may update this notice to reflect product changes, relevant legal requirements and company practices. Material changes will be communicated where required.
