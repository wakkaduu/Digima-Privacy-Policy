Your information and your choices

If you contacted a Facebook Page that uses Digima Messenger CRM, you may have rights concerning information in that conversation, subject to applicable law and the role of the business operating that Page.

Important: The official privacy-request contact and end-to-end deletion process have not yet been confirmed by Digima. This page is a draft and must not be represented as an operational deletion service until approved.

Proposed request procedure

  1. Find the correct contact. A company-approved privacy contact channel must be published here before launch. Contact email pending
  2. Describe the Facebook Page and conversation. Include the Page name and an approximate date to help locate the relevant conversation. Avoid sending passwords, tokens or unnecessary sensitive details.
  3. Verify your identity when requested. Reasonable identity checks may be needed to protect against fraudulent or unauthorized deletion requests.
  4. Receive a response. The responsible business will assess the request, determine applicable rights and lawful retention exceptions, and provide a response through the confirmed process.

What can happen to the information?

Depending on the request, applicable laws, and the business's responsibilities, information may be provided, corrected, deleted, blocked or retained where lawful obligations require it. Deleting CRM data does not automatically delete copies held independently by Facebook/Meta or another business.

Digima must verify how requests propagate to message storage, attachments, logs and backups before claiming a particular completion time or method.

Response target, deletion process and backup handling: company approval required

Protect your information

Never send passwords, login codes, Page access tokens, payment card numbers or government ID photographs through an unverified contact form. Use only an official channel published by the responsible business.
