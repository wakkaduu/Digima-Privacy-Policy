Where to send a privacy request

Before public publication, Digima will need to approve an email address or other working contact channel for personal-data requests and specify who is responsible for responding.

For conversations with a Page operated by a separate business, the Page owner may also be responsible for handling the request. The responsible party must be determined from the actual relationship.

No contact form is active on this website. The page does not collect or send your personal information. Please do not share personal information with an unverified destination.

Privacy and safety reminder

Only disclose the minimum information necessary to explain your request, and never provide passwords or account access tokens. For more information, read the Privacy Policy and Data Deletion Instructions.
